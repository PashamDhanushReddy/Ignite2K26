Rules & Criteria

Participation Guidelines

Everything you need to know before registering your project team.

Eligibility

Students from all Engineering, Polytechnic, MBA, and MCA colleges across India are eligible to participate.

Team Size

Projects must be submitted in teams of minimum 2 to maximum 6 students with one designated leader.

Working Prototype

Each entry must present a live prototype, simulation, or functional design model on expo day.

Certificates

Official Merit & Participation Certificates with college seal and autonomous validation for all registered attendees.

Attendee Perks

Official customized event T-Shirt, delegate kit, lunch & snacks provided for every registered participant.

Registration Fee

Rs. 250/- per student

Deadline: 16-02-2027
